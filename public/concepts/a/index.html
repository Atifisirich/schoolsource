An order in four steps

  1. 1Send us your list

    On WhatsApp, by email, or as a photo of a handwritten page. Last year’s order works as a list too.

  2. 2Get one quotation

    Every category on your list, on one quotation, for the quantities you need. Printing starts only after you approve the proof.

  3. 3We check and pack

    Every order is checked before dispatch. Student kits go in one labelled bag per student, sorted by class.

  4. 4Delivered to your school

    In-stock items arrive within a week of confirmation, by our own team or a transporter, in Hyderabad and other states.

What comes with every order

One team handles your school from the first list to the last carton.

  1. School visits with samples

    We bring the products to your school so you can see and hold them before you order.

  2. Proof before printing

    Diaries, ID cards and certificates are printed only after you approve the proof.

  3. Replaced if wrong

    Damaged, short or wrong items are replaced.

  4. Last year’s order, quoted again

    Send last year’s list and we quote it for this year.

  5. School groups with branches

    One supplier for every branch of your group.

  6. Stock in our warehouse

    Everyday items are held in stock, so term-start orders go out on time.
